# Arwa Mostafa - Week 9 Progress

### Overall Progress

This week, I developed a new microprocessor card for our Human Interface that allows students to view the pinouts of different microprocessors, understand the functionality of each pin, and access important information and considerations when working with each board. I added several commonly used microprocessor boards to the card so students can easily find the information they need during their labs. This feature helps make the interface more useful for students working with different hardware components.

### Current Blockers

My main blocker is figuring out how to allow TAs to add their own microprocessor boards to the card. Currently, I have manually added a selection of commonly used boards, but I am still exploring how to make this feature customizable so TAs can add boards specific to their courses.

### Plans for Next Week

Next week, I plan to explore and implement a way for TAs to add their own microprocessor boards, including their pinout diagrams, pin descriptions, and important information. I also plan to help with integrating QueUp into our interface once we have discussed the integration process with its developer.
