### Overall Progress

This week, our team worked on preparing and presenting the Human Interface presentation to the other subteams in our class. We discussed our final solution and explained its main features and functionality. These include a chat interface where students can ask questions and upload lab manuals and images, conversation history that allows students to access their previous chats, a lab checklist to help students track their progress, and a resistor color-code tool. We also presented our student authentication system, which allows students to log in to their accounts and access their saved conversations, along with the TA dashboard designed to help TAs monitor student progress and manage help requests.

### Current Blockers

We have no major blockers at the moment but we are still exploring database deployment options and awaiting clarification regarding Purdue SSO integration and FERPA requirements.

### Plans for Next Week

Next week, we plan to explore integrating QueUp into our interface to improve communication and help-request management between students and TAs. We also aim to make our interface more modular so it can be adapted for multiple courses rather than being limited to one. Additionally, we plan to expand our component tools by adding support for other electrical components, such as capacitor and inductor codes, alongside the existing resistor color-code tool.
