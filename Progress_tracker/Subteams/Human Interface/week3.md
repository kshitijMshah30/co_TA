# Human Interface Team - Week 3 Progress

## Overall Progress

This week, each team member developed their own UI prototype for the project. We then compared our designs and discussed the features we liked most from each prototype. After agreeing on the best features, we combined them into a single UI design. Here is our final demo for this week: https://vip-ai-ta-demo.netlify.app/

## Current Blockers

One is coordination with the Intelligence team. We need to agree on what context they expect from our team, the format in which it should be sent, and what response format we should expect back. We also need to make sure the reasoning loop supports the main goal of the AI TA: helping students understand lab concepts, procedures, equipment, and errors without directly giving them answers.

## Plans for Next Week

We divided the following tasks among our team members: saving conversation history, sending context (lab manual, images, student questions, and conversation history) to the Intelligence team, displaying the AI-generated response in our UI, image tracing, and developing the reasoning loop. Each team member will work on their assigned task, and our goal is to have all of these components completed by our next team meeting. Our current focus is on defining how these components will work
individually and how they will communicate with the other project
