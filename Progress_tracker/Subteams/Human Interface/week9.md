### Overall Progress

This week, our team successfully implemented the modular design of our Human Interface, allowing it to support multiple courses rather than being limited to one. TAs can now create new courses and customize their interfaces by enabling or disabling specific features and cards based on their course requirements. They can also upload lab materials, set a class password, and provide lab steps and learning objectives for students to follow. Students can join multiple courses using the appropriate class passwords and access the lab materials uploaded by their TAs, while being restricted from uploading their own lab materials. Additionally, we developed a new microprocessor card that displays the pinouts of different microprocessors, explains the functionality of each pin, and highlights important information and considerations students should keep in mind when working with them. These improvements make our interface more flexible and adaptable to the needs of different courses.

### Current Blockers

Our main blocker is the integration of QueUp into our interface. We are currently waiting for a meeting with the QueUp developer to discuss the integration process and determine how to connect it to our existing system.

### Plans for Next Week

Next week, we plan to begin integrating QueUp into our interface after meeting with its developer. We also plan to implement a feature that allows TAs to start and end sessions for individual labs. At the end of each session, the TA will receive a summary of how students interacted with the interface, providing insight into their progress and the areas where they needed assistance. Additionally, we plan to develop a new card that helps students identify capacitor and inductor codes, expanding the tools available within the interface to support different lab activities.
